# 🤖 Robotics & Drones Club — MSEC

Welcome to the official repository for the **Robotics & Drones Club** at **Meenakshi Sundararajan Engineering College (MSEC)**.

> **Build. Innovate. Engineer.**  
> A build-first robotics ecosystem turning students into engineers who ship working systems.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Objectives

- 🛠️ **Hands-on Hardware & Software Projects**: Autonomous drones, embedded systems, microcontrollers, ROS, and computer vision.
- 🏆 **Hackathons & Competitions**: Preparing students for national & international robotics leagues.
- 💡 **Workshops & Bootcamps**: Interactive training on circuit design, drone assembly, 3D printing, and IoT.
